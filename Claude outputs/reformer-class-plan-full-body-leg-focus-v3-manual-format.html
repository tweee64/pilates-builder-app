FULL BODY, LEG FOCUS — V3 THE CIRCLE
PROPS: BALL, SHORT BOX

Warm up and lower body:

Spring: RR (footbar up, ball on the floor by the shoulder blocks)

Lying supine – feet on footbar, hips width apart

  • Breathwork – lateral thoracic
  • Cat/Cow on the carriage, breath-led x 5-8

Ball between thighs

  • Bridge Roll + ball squeeze x 5-8
    • Heel lifts at the top x 5-8

Ball away

  • Double Leg Press, easy flowing tempo x 10-12

Lower body — footwork:

Spring: RRR (RR to regress, to be changed during sequence)

Lying supine – footbar up, heaviest block of the class

  • Double Leg Press Series
    • Heels x 10-12
    • Toes/prehensile x 10-12
    • Wide/turnout x 8-10 slow – give this real time
  • Heel Raises, slow eccentric x 10-12
  • Prances x 10-12 each side

Drop to RR

  • Single Leg Press, free leg tabletop x 10-12 each side
    • + pulses anywhere

Roll up to standing, drop spring to single R, hands light on the footbar

Lower body — standing, right side:

Spring: R (RR to regress / B to advance — holds for the next 17 minutes)

Standing foot on the platform, working foot on the carriage, facing the reformer

  • Long Lunge R x 60-75 sec – the on-ramp, slow and grounded
  • Scooter R, hips square x 60-75 sec
  • Fly Lunge R x 60-75 sec
  • Sink into Long Lunge and hold x 20 sec

Step down, lie back on the carriage, collect the ball. Machine unchanged.

Core:

Spring: R (unchanged — footbar still up, feet on the bar)

Supine, ball in the hands or between the thighs

  • Hundred – heels on footbar or tabletop, ball between thighs x 100 beats
  • Chest Lift + Oblique reach past opposite knee x 10-12 each side
  • Roll Back and Hover – seated facing footbar, feet pressing the bar x 8-10
  • Bridge on the footbar + ball squeeze x 8-10
    • Single leg bridge to advance x 6-8 each side

Ball down, back up to standing, other side. Machine unchanged.

Lower body — standing, left side + pike:

Spring: R (unchanged)

Repeat the right-side series, same order, same tempo

  • Long Lunge L x 60-75 sec
  • Scooter L, hips square x 60-75 sec
  • Fly Lunge L x 60-75 sec – the peak of the class
  • Sink into Long Lunge and hold x 20 sec

Both feet onto the carriage, hands stay on the footbar

  • Plank hold x 20-30 sec
  • Single Knee Tuck, alternating x 10-12
  • Full Pike, hips high x 8-10
    • Hold the last one and breathe x 20 sec

Footbar down, spring to B, straps in your hands. Last change of the class.

Upper body:

Spring: B (Y to regress)

Kneeling, facing the footbar, straps from behind

  • Arms Pull Back, straight arms x 10-12
  • Chest Expansion + head turns on the hold x 8-10
  • Tricep Press Back, elbows pinned x 10-12

Box out, turn, seated on the short box facing the risers

  • Seated Row x 12-16
  • Reverse Fly x 10-12
  • Bicep Curls x 10-12 + pulses
    • Teaser off the box x 5-8 – optional, strong groups only

Box stays out for the mermaid

Cool down

Spring: B (unchanged)

  • Mermaid, seated on the box x 3-5 each side

Box away

  • Kneeling Hip Flexor / Quad Stretch x 3-5 breaths each side

Lie back on the carriage

  • Supine Figure-4 x 3-5 breaths each side
  • Supine Spinal Twist x 3-5 breaths each side

Ball resting on the belly

  • Breathwork
