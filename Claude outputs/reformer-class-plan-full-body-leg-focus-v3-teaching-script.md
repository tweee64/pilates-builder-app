# Teaching Script — Full Body, Leg Focus (v3 · The Circle)

**~48 min door to door** (2 min settle + 46 min class) · Mixed level · Group reformer
**Props:** small ball · short box · straps (hands only)

---

## How to use this script

- **Lines in quotes are what you say.** Say them out loud in your own mouth-shape — don't read them. If a line feels like someone else's sentence, cut it.
- **`[DO]`** = what your hands and feet are doing: setup, demo, spring changes, walking the room.
- **`[SCAN]`** = the one or two things to look for in that exercise. Not a checklist to recite — a thing to look for and fix on one or two bodies, by name.
- **`[SILENCE]`** = stop talking. These are deliberate. A class where the teacher never stops talking never lets anyone feel anything.
- Rep counts are written as ranges. **Count down, never up** — "three, two, one" tells people the end is real. "Five, six, seven" tells them nothing.
- Anything marked *optional* can be cut without the class noticing. If you're running long, cut from the **arm block** (curls, teaser) and the **prances**, never from the cooldown.

**Timing spine — glance at the clock at these four moments only:**

| Clock | You should be |
|---|---|
| **7:00** | Starting footwork (RRR) |
| **15:00** | Standing, right side |
| **25:00** | Standing, left side |
| **32:00** | Footbar down, blue spring, straps |

If you're more than 90 seconds behind at any of those, trim reps in the block you're in — don't rush the transition.

---

# 0:00 — Welcome & setup (~2 min)

`[DO]` Stand where the whole room can see you. Ball already on the floor by your shoulder blocks. Short box stacked wherever it lives — you don't need it for 32 minutes.

> "Welcome in, everybody. Come and sit on your carriage for me — you don't need anything yet."

> "Today is a full body class with a leg focus. Two props: a small ball, which you'll find on the floor by your shoulder blocks, and the short box, which we don't touch until the very end."

> "Before we start — anything I should know? Anything sore, anything new, anyone pregnant or recovering from anything?"

`[DO]` Wait. Actually wait. Make eye contact with anyone new. Note who told you what — you'll use it in the pike and the lunges.

> "Two things about how today runs."

> "One: **the movement is the same for all of us today.** I'm not going to give you three different exercises. What's yours to choose is your spring and your range. That's where this class gets easier or harder."

> "Two: your machine barely changes. Three spring changes, all in the first half. After about the thirty minute mark, nothing on your reformer moves again."

> "Springs to start: **two reds**. Footbar up. Ball on the floor where you can reach it."

`[DO]` Walk the room. Check every carriage. This is the last easy chance to correct a spring.

---

# 2:00 — WARM-UP & MOBILITY (~5 min)
### 🔴🔴 RR · footbar **up** · ball nearby

**Purpose:** breath, spine, wake the legs. Plant the two motifs — *the breath* and *the ball*.

### Breathwork — supine, feet on the footbar

`[DO]` Lie down on your own machine for this one if you can see the room from there. Model the breath rather than describing it.

> "Lie all the way back. Feet up onto the footbar, hips width apart, heels together-ish — just find a place that feels neutral. Arms long by your sides."

> "Let the back of your head be heavy. Let your shoulders be heavy. Nothing to do yet."

> "Hands onto your ribs for a moment — thumbs behind, fingers wrapping around the front."

> "**Inhale** — send the breath sideways into your hands. Wide, not up. Feel the ribs open under your fingers."

> "**Exhale** — all the way out. Let the ribs draw back together and down."

> "Again. Inhale wide… and exhale, everything out."

`[SILENCE]` Three or four full breaths. Say nothing.

> "That's the breath for the whole class. Whenever you lose it — and you will, somewhere around the lunges — that's the one to come back to."

### Cat / Cow on the carriage — breath-led, x5–8

> "Arms stay long. Feet stay on the bar."

> "**Exhale** — tip your pelvis towards you, tail curls up, low back presses into the carriage. Ribs soften down."

> "**Inhale** — tip your pelvis the other way, tail reaches away, a little arch through the low back, chest opens."

> "And keep going with your own breath. No counting from me."

`[SILENCE]` Let them find their own rhythm for 5–8 cycles. Only speak to make it bigger:

> "Bigger than you think. Take up the whole range you've got."

> "And find the middle again — neutral. Pelvis level, low back neither pressed nor arched."

`[SCAN]` The people moving fastest are the ones not breathing. Slow them by slowing your own voice, not by telling them.

### Bridge + ball squeeze — x5–8 *(this comes back in the core block)*

> "Reach down and grab your ball. Ball between your thighs, up high, closer to the hips than the knees."

> "Gently hug the ball — not a squeeze, a hug. Feel the inner thighs answer."

> "**Inhale** to prepare. **Exhale** — curl the tail up first, then peel the spine off the carriage one vertebra at a time. Like lifting a string of pearls, one pearl at a time."

> "Top of your bridge. Hips high, ball still hugged."

> "**Inhale** at the top. **Exhale** — melt back down, top of the spine first, one pearl at a time, tail last."

> "And again — exhale to peel up…"

`[DO]` Give 5–8. Then, at the top of the last one:

> "Stay up at the top this time. Hips high."

**Heel lifts at the top — x5–8**

> "Now lift both heels — press through the balls of the feet, hips stay high. And lower. Lift… and lower."

> "That's your calves saying hello. We'll come back for them properly."

> "Last three. Three… two… one. And melt the whole spine down. Ball out — pop it back on the floor by your shoulder blocks. You'll want it again later."

`[SCAN]` Bridges where the ribs flare and the low back does all the work. Cue: *"start with the tail, not the ribs."*

### Double leg press — easy flowing tempo, x10–12

> "Feet back on the bar, hips width. Heels on the bar, toes relaxed."

> "This one's just heat. Easy tempo, nothing heroic."

> "**Exhale** press out — long legs, don't lock. **Inhale** bend in — and control the carriage home. Don't let it drop."

> "Out… and in. Out… and in."

> "Ten of these. And they should feel easy, because they are."

> "Last three. Three… two… one. Carriage in, bend the knees, and **come up to sitting for me.**"

`⚙ [DO] SPRING CHANGE — add a third red. 🔴🔴🔴 RRR.`

> "Add one more red — three reds. Footbar stays up. This is the heaviest we go all class, and it's now, while you're fresh."

---

# 7:00 — SECTION 1 · FOOTWORK FOUNDATIONS (~8 min)
### 🔴🔴🔴 RRR · footbar **up** · no props

**Purpose:** bilateral, heavy legs in the most stable position of the day.

### Double leg press — heels · x10–12

> "Lie back. Heels on the bar, hips width apart, parallel."

> "Same breath. Exhale out, inhale in. But heavier now — so slower."

> "Press from the **whole foot**, not just the ball of the foot. And **resist the carriage home** — the return is the half people waste."

> "Out… and resist in. Out… and resist."

> "Halfway. Keep the pelvis level — hip bones facing the ceiling."

> "Last four. Four, three, two… and one. Hold the carriage out."

### Double leg press — toes / prehensile · x10–12

> "Stay out. Roll onto the balls of your feet — heels lift up above the bar, toes wrap the bar if that's available to you."

> "Heels stay high the whole time — that's the work."

> "Exhale out… inhale in. Knees track over the middle toes."

> "Ten. And the heels don't drop. Don't let them drop."

> "Last three. Three, two, one. Hold out."

`[SCAN]` Heels sinking below the bar between reps. That's the whole exercise leaking away.

### Double leg press — wide / turnout · x8–10 **slow**

`[DO]` **Give this real time.** This is the only dedicated adductor loading before the ball work later. Don't let it become a token four reps.

> "Stay out. Feet wide on the bar — out to the corners. Heels on the bar, toes turned out."

> "And the turnout comes **from the hip, not the knee.** Rotate the whole thigh open."

> "This one is slow. Slower than you want."

> "**Exhale** press out — feel the inner thighs join in at the end of the press. **Inhale** bend in — knees travel out over your toes, wide."

> "Out… slow. In… slow."

> "Eight of these. Feel the inner thigh. That's the bit we're buying here."

> "Last three, and slower again. Three… two… one. Hold out."

### Heel raises — slow eccentric · x10–12

> "Feet back to parallel, hips width, balls of the feet on the bar. Legs stay long — knees soft, not locked."

> "Now just the ankles. **Lift** the heels — press up to the highest point over your big toe. **Lower** — slow, and take the heel below the bar line if you've got it."

> "Up… and slow all the way down. Up… and slooooow down."

> "The lowering is the exercise. Don't bounce."

> "Last four. Four… three… two… one. Bend the knees, carriage in."

### Prances · x10–12 each side *(cut first if running long)*

> "Balls of both feet on the bar. Press out to long legs."

> "Now alternate — one heel drops down and under as the opposite knee bends. Like walking on the spot. Rhythm."

> "Down and under… and change. And change."

> "Keep it even — same tempo both sides."

> "Ten each. Last four… three, two, one. Both heels down, carriage in."

`⚙ [DO] SPRING CHANGE — drop one red. 🔴🔴 RR.`

> "Drop one red for me — two reds. One leg shouldn't have to carry three."

### Single leg press — free leg tabletop · x10–12 each side

> "Right foot on the bar, hips width from centre — not in the middle, in *your* lane."

> "Left leg comes up to tabletop — knee over hip, shin parallel to the ceiling. Or, if the back is talking to you, keep that left foot down on the bar and just let the right leg do the work."

> "**Both hip bones facing the ceiling. Level, like a tabletop.** That's the whole exercise — the leg is easy, keeping the pelvis honest is not."

> "Exhale press out… inhale resist in."

> "Ten. And I'm watching your hips, not your leg."

> "Last three, three, two, one — and hold out. **Small pulses.** Ten. Just an inch. Ten, nine, eight…"

> "Carriage in. Change legs."

`[DO]` Repeat left. **Talk less on the second side** — they know it now. Use the time to walk the room and fix one pelvis with your hands.

> "Last three. Three, two, one. Both feet back to the bar, carriage in. Bend the knees. And **come all the way up to standing.**"

`⚙ [DO] SPRING CHANGE — down to one red. 🔴 R.`

> "Springs down to **one red**. Footbar stays up. Hands light on the bar."

> "Now listen, because this next bit matters: **whatever spring you pick right now stays with you for the next seventeen minutes** — through the lunges, through the core, and through the other side. So pick honestly."

> "**One red** is our base. If you want more support — if balance is the hard part today — **go to two reds** and put a real hand on that bar. Heavier actually helps you here, it pins the carriage down."

> "If you want it harder, **go to blue.** Lighter carriage means more range and a lot more work to stay still. Choose."

`[DO]` Give them ten seconds to actually change it. Then, before anyone moves:

> "And here's the shape of the next twenty minutes so you can commit to it: **three moves on the right. Then we lie down for a bit. Then we match it on the left. Then one thing together.** That's it. Knowing the lie-down is coming means you can afford to spend on this first side."

# 15:00 — SECTION 2 · STANDING LEGS, RIGHT SIDE (~5 min)
### 🔴 R · footbar **up** · facing the reformer

**Everything here faces the reformer, hands on the footbar. One direction, no resets.**

`[DO]` Demo the base position once, quickly, then get off and teach with your voice and hands.

> "Come round to face your reformer. **Right foot goes onto the carriage**, up by the shoulder blocks. **Left foot on the platform** — the non-moving bit."

> "Hands light on the footbar. And I mean light — **the footbar is a balance point, not a handle. Fingertips, not fists.** Unless you need it as an anchor, in which case grip it and mean it. That's a real option."

### Long lunge, right · ~60–75 sec

**This is the on-ramp. Slow, grounded, teaches the shape.**

> "Bend both knees a little. Front knee over the middle of your foot — not past your toes."

> "**Exhale** — slide that back leg long behind you. Let the carriage travel. Feel the front of that right hip open."

> "**Inhale** — draw it home. Control it. No slamming."

> "Long… and home. Long… and home."

> "Chest stays lifted. You're not folding forward, you're lengthening back."

> "And keep going — find your own range. If the low back arches, make the range smaller. Smaller range, not more grit."

`[SCAN]` Front knee diving inside the foot. Cue by name: *"Sarah — knee out over your second toe."*

> "Twenty more seconds. Same tempo."

> "And bring it home. Stay standing — **don't come out of the position.**"

### Scooter, right · ~60–75 sec

**Precision. Shorter range on purpose.**

> "Same feet. Now the back leg stays a bit more bent and presses **straight back** — shorter range than the lunge. This isn't about distance."

> "**Square your hips forward like headlights.** Both pointing at the wall. That hip wants to open — don't let it."

> "And switch on the glute of your **standing** leg. The one that isn't moving. That's the hidden half."

> "Exhale press back… inhale in. Small. Precise."

> "Press… and in. Press… and in."

> "Shorter than you want. Squarer than you want."

`[SCAN]` Open hips — the working-side hip rotating out. Most common fault in the class.

> "Fifteen more seconds. Hips square. Glute on."

> "And in. Stay standing."

### Fly lunge, right · ~60–75 sec

**Power. This is the loud one.**

> "Same shape as the long lunge — but now **dynamic**, and light through that back leg."

> "Bigger range. Faster. **Push out with power, come home with control.** Power out, control in."

> "And go. Out… and in. Out… and in."

> "Breathe. Whatever pattern you've got, keep breathing."

> "This is real work. Stay with it."

> "Fifteen seconds. This is the bit you'll be glad you did."

> "Ten… and five, four, three, two, one."

### Sink and hold · 20 sec

**Don't come out of it. The stretch is built into the work.**

> "Now — **don't come out.** Sink down into a long lunge and stay there."

> "Back leg long, front knee bent, hips sinking down and forward."

> "Feel the front of that right hip open. That's the hip flexor you just worked, getting released in the exact position you're already standing in."

> "And breathe. Twenty seconds."

`[SILENCE]` Count it in your head. Say nothing for fifteen of them.

> "Five more breaths' worth… and come out of it. Well done. That side's finished."

### `[DO]` Transition out — **nothing on the machine moves**

> "Step off. Come and lie back on the carriage — head up by the shoulder blocks. **And grab your ball on the way down.**"

> "Don't touch your springs. Don't touch the bar. It all stays exactly as it is."

---

# 20:00 — SECTION 3 · CORE (~5 min)
### 🔴 R · footbar **up** · ball

**The supine reset in the middle of the circle. Real work, but it reads as relief.**

`[DO]` **The footbar is still up — use it.** Feet on the bar turn the carriage into the feedback mechanism. *"The carriage doesn't move"* beats any cue you can invent about navels, because they can feel it instantly.

> "Lie all the way back. Feet up onto the footbar."

> "Here's the rule for this whole block: **the carriage doesn't move.** If it moves, your pressure is uneven. That's my only core cue today, and you don't need me to check it — you can hear it."

### Hundred · 100 beats

> "Ball between your thighs, up high. Hug it."

> "Heels on the bar, legs long — **or** feet flat and knees bent, whichever your neck and back prefer. Both are correct."

> "Arms long by your hips, floating a few inches off the carriage."

> "**Inhale** — curl your head and chest up, eyes to your thighs, arms reach long past your hips."

> "And pump. **Inhale for five** — one, two, three, four, five. **Exhale for five** — one, two, three, four, five."

> "That's ten. Ten more like it."

`[DO]` Count the sets out loud — *"twenty… thirty… forty…"* Landmarks keep people in.

> "Fifty. Halfway. Ribs soft, carriage still, ball hugged."

> "Sixty… seventy… eighty…"

> "Last twenty. Twenty. Ten. And… down. Head down, arms down, take a breath."

`[SCAN]` Necks. If someone's chin is jamming or their head is shaking, send them down: *"head down, keep the arms pumping — that's the same exercise."*

### Chest lift + oblique reach · x10–12 each side

> "Ball into your hands. Feet stay on the bar."

> "**Exhale** — curl up, and **reach the ball past the outside of your left knee.** Twist from the ribs, not the arms."

> "**Inhale** — back to centre and down, just a little."

> "**Exhale** — up and reach past the **right** knee."

> "And alternate. Left… and right. Left… and right."

> "**And the carriage doesn't move.** Press evenly through both feet — even while you twist."

> "Ten each side. Last three each way… three… two… one. And all the way down."

### Roll back and hover · x8–10

**The useful half of a teaser, without the exposure.**

> "Come up to sitting. Turn to **face the footbar** — sit tall, knees bent, **feet pressing into the footbar.**"

> "Ball in your hands, arms reaching forward at about chest height. The ball's your target."

> "Now listen — this is the important bit. **Press your heels into that bar as you come back up.** Let your hamstrings help. If your hamstrings do the work, your hip flexors get to stay quiet — and you're going to want them back for the other leg."

> "**Exhale** — roll back. Tail curls under, spine rolls down one bone at a time, until you're **hovering** — not lying down. Somewhere in the middle."

> "**Inhale** at the hover. **Exhale** — press those heels and roll all the way back up, reaching the ball forward."

> "Roll back… hover… and press up."

> "If the hover's too much, **go halfway and come back.** That's the same exercise and it's the smart one."

> "And if you want more — take it all the way down and roll all the way up. Your call."

> "Eight of them. Last three. Three… two… one. And lie all the way back down."

`[SCAN]` People throwing their arms to get up. Cue: *"heels into the bar — let the legs do it."*

### Bridge on the footbar + ball squeeze · x8–10
### *(single leg to advance, x6–8 each side)*

**Ends the block on hip extension — re-primes the glutes and opens the hip flexors before the second leg side. The reset isn't just rest, it's the on-ramp.**

> "Feet on the bar, hips width. Ball between the thighs. **We started class here — this is where it comes back.**"

> "**Exhale** — squeeze the ball, curl the tail, peel up. String of pearls."

> "Top of the bridge, hips high, ball hugged."

> "**Inhale** at the top. **Exhale** — melt down, one pearl at a time."

> "And again. Squeeze… peel up… and melt down."

> "That squeeze is doing real work, by the way — those are your inner thighs, and this is the second time today we've asked them."

> "If you want more: **take one foot off** and bridge on a single leg. Same tempo, level hips. Six each side. Only if the pelvis stays honest."

> "Last three together. Three… two… one. And melt all the way down."

### `[DO]` Transition out — **nothing on the machine moves**

> "Ball down on the floor. Come up to standing. **Other side.**"

> "And again — don't touch your spring. It's still yours from before."

# 25:00 — SECTION 4 · STANDING LEGS, LEFT SIDE + PIKE (~7 min)
### 🔴 R · footbar **up**

## Left side (~5 min)

**Same three, same order, same spring. `[DO]` Talk noticeably less. Cue quality and breath, not setup.**

> "Face your reformer. **Left foot on the carriage**, right foot on the platform. Hands light on the bar."

> "You know the shapes now, so I'm going to talk less. Two things before we go:"

> "**Same tempo as the right side.** There's a real instinct to rush side two to get it over with. We're not in a hurry."

> "And **expect this side to feel different.** One side always feels stronger. That's information, not a problem."

### Long lunge, left · ~60–75 sec

> "Bend both knees. Exhale — slide the back leg long. Inhale — control it home."

> "Long… and home."

`[SILENCE]` Then, sparingly:

> "Chest lifted. Knee over the second toe. Breathe."

> "Twenty seconds. And home — stay standing."

### Scooter, left · ~60–75 sec

> "Back leg bends, presses straight back. Shorter range. **Hips square like headlights.** Standing glute on."

> "Press… and in."

`[SILENCE]`

> "Squarer. Shorter. Precise."

> "Fifteen seconds. And in — stay standing."

### Fly lunge, left · ~60–75 sec

**Name the peak.**

> "Last one. And I'll be honest with you: **this is the hardest thing we do today.** Right here. It's all downhill from here — so spend it."

> "Dynamic, big range, light through the back leg. **Power out, control in.** And go."

> "Out… and in. Out… and in."

> "Breathe. Stay with it. This is the one."

> "Fifteen seconds — biggest range you've got."

> "Ten… five, four, three, two, one."

### Sink and hold · 20 sec

> "**Don't come out.** Sink down into your long lunge and stay."

> "Back leg long, hips sinking. Open the front of that left hip."

> "Breathe into it. That's your reward and it's the same position."

`[SILENCE]` Fifteen seconds of nothing.

> "And come out. **That's the legs done.** Both sides, matched. Nice work."

## Pike — the close (~2 min)

`[DO]` Same spring, same bar, same direction — they're already facing it. This is the hinge into the arm block: they finish kneeling-ready.

> "Stay facing the reformer. **Both feet come onto the carriage now**, up by the shoulder blocks. Hands stay on the footbar."

> "This is a ladder. **Everybody starts at rung one.** Climb as far as you own it and stop there — there's no prize for the top."

### Rung 1 — Plank hold · 20–30 sec

> "Both feet on the carriage, hands on the bar, body long. **Plank.**"

> "**Shoulders out of your ears** — slide the shoulder blades down your back. Heels reaching back, crown of the head reaching forward."

> "Hold it. Breathe. Twenty seconds."

> "If your wrists or the front of your shoulder don't like this, **come down onto your forearms on the footbar** — or step off and give me standing calf raises instead. Both are fine, and I'd rather you did either than push through."

> "Ten… five, four, three, two, one."

### Rung 2 — Single knee tuck, alternating · x10–12

> "Stay in the plank. **Exhale** — draw one knee in towards your chest, carriage comes in. **Inhale** — press it back out to plank."

> "And change. In… and out. In… and out."

> "Hips stay level. Shoulders stay out of your ears."

> "Ten. Last four… three, two, one. Back to your plank."

### Rung 3 — Full pike · x8–10

> "If you want the top rung: **both feet together, exhale, hips lift high** — send the tail to the ceiling, carriage draws in. Legs as straight as you've got."

> "**Inhale** — lower back down to your long plank."

> "Hips high… and long. High… and long."

> "And if you're staying in the plank, or on the knee tucks — perfect. Stay there."

> "Last three. Three… two… one."

### Hold the last one · 20 sec

> "**Hold your last one.** Whichever rung you're on — plank, tuck, or pike. Hold it and breathe."

> "If you're in the pike, feel the back of your legs — that's your hamstrings and calves lengthening. **The reward for the leg work is the leg work.**"

> "Breathe. Ten more seconds."

> "Five, four, three, two, one — and come down. Step off and give your wrists a shake."

### `⚙ [DO] LAST CHANGE OF THE CLASS`

> "Okay — **footbar down. Springs to one blue. Straps in your hands.**"

> "Take a breath while you do it. **We're changing gears** — the legs are done. Everything from here is arms, and then we stretch."

> "And this is the **last change of the class.** Nothing on your machine moves again."

`[DO]` Walk the room. Check footbars are actually down and springs are actually blue — this is the change people get wrong because they're tired.

# 32:00 — SECTION 5 · BACK + TRICEPS (~11 min)
### 🔵 B · footbar **down** · straps + short box

**Purpose:** the postural counter. The pike just loaded the front of the shoulders, the triceps and the wrists — so this block *pulls*.

**Say this once and mean it:**

> "**Melt your shoulder blades down into your back pockets before you pull.** Every exercise in this block. If your shoulders creep up towards your ears, that's the only thing going wrong."

> "And lighten to **yellow** any time you want. Lighter is not losing — sloppy is losing."

## Position A — kneeling, facing the footbar (~5 min)

`[DO]` They stay exactly where the pike left them. Straps come from behind. **No box yet.**

> "Kneel on the carriage, facing the footbar — knees roughly hip width, back towards the risers."

> "Straps in your hands, coming from behind you. Sit up tall — **ribs stacked over hips**, don't arch."

> "Pad under your knees if you want one, grab it now."

### Arms pull back — straight arms · x10–12

> "Arms long in front of you at about shoulder height. Straight arms — they stay straight the whole time."

> "**Exhale** — draw the straight arms **down and back** towards your hips. Feel it under your armpits — that's your lats."

> "**Inhale** — float them back up in front. Slowly. **The straps want to pull you back. Don't let them win.**"

> "Down and back… and slowly up."

> "Shoulder blades into the back pockets first. Then pull."

> "Ten. Last three… three, two, one."

### Chest expansion + head turns · x8–10

> "Arms come down long by your sides, palms facing back."

> "**Exhale** — press both arms straight back behind you. Open the collarbones wide. **And hold.**"

> "Stay there. Now — **turn your head to look over your right shoulder.** Centre. **Left shoulder.** Centre."

> "And release the arms forward."

> "Again — press back, hold, lengthen the collarbones wide, breathe into the front of your chest…"

> "This should feel enormous. You've been facing forward and pressing things away from you for thirty minutes."

> "Eight. Last three… three, two, one."

### Tricep press back — elbows pinned · x10–12

**Zero transition. Same arm line, just add the elbow bend.**

> "Don't move. **Same position** — press the arms back to that hold."

> "Now **pin your elbows exactly where they are** and bend them. Hands come forward towards your shoulders."

> "**Exhale** — straighten the arms back. **Inhale** — bend."

> "**Only the forearm moves.** Elbows stay pinned. That's the whole exercise."

> "Press… and bend. Press… and bend."

> "Ten. Last four… three, two, one. And release the arms forward. Come off your knees."

## Position B — seated on the short box (~6 min)

`[DO]` Box out, one turn, sit down. Sitting after kneeling reads as a break even though the work continues.

> "Straps down for a second. **Grab your short box** and set it on the carriage."

> "Sit on the box **facing the risers** — so facing your straps. Feet down on the frame or the floor, wherever's stable. Sit tall."

> "**Box stays out after this** — we use it again in the stretch. Don't put it away."

> "Straps back in your hands."

### Seated row · x12–16

> "Arms long in front, about chest height. Palms facing each other or down, your choice."

> "**Exhale** — pull the elbows straight back past your ribs. **Squeeze the shoulder blades together** behind you."

> "**Inhale** — reach the arms long again, slowly."

> "Pull… and reach. Pull… and reach."

> "Tall spine. Don't rock back and forth — the spine stays still, only the arms move."

> "Twelve. Last four… three, two, one."

### Reverse fly · x10–12

> "Arms come up to shoulder height in front of you, straight, palms facing down."

> "**Exhale** — open the arms **wide** out to the sides, like you're opening a big pair of curtains. Feel the back of the shoulders."

> "**Inhale** — bring them back together in front. Slowly."

> "Open… and together. Open… and together."

> "Shoulders down. If they're creeping, **go to yellow** — this is the one that needs lighter."

> "Ten. Last three… three, two, one."

### Bicep curls · x10–12 + pulses

**The dessert. ~90 seconds. Ends the working part of class on a pump.**

> "Last one of the arm block. Turn the palms up to face the ceiling, elbows into your sides."

> "**Exhale** — curl the hands in towards your shoulders. **Inhale** — lower, slowly. Elbows stay glued to your ribs."

> "Curl… and lower. Curl… and lower."

> "Ten. Last four, three, two, one — **and hold halfway.**"

> "**Pulses.** Small. Ten. Ten, nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two, one."

> "And release the straps. Shake the arms out. **That's the work done.**"

### *Optional — Teaser off the box · x5–8 · strong groups only*

`[DO]` **Cut this freely.** The class does not need it. Only offer it if the room is strong and the clock is kind — you should be at 41:00 or earlier.

> "If you've got one more in you, and only if you do: **teaser off the box.** Sit tall, lean back, float the legs up to a V, arms reaching long."

> "Five. Legs are done for the day anyway, so there's nothing left to protect."

> "And down. Everyone else — well done for sitting this one out, that's a real skill."

# 43:00 — COOL DOWN (~5 min)
### 🔵 B · footbar **down** · box (then away) · ball

**Descend through the positions — seated, kneeling, supine. End horizontal.**

> "Springs stay blue. Nothing changes for the rest of class. The spring is just support now."

### Mermaid, seated on the box · x3–5 each side

> "Stay on your box. Turn to sit sideways on it — face the long side of your reformer."

> "One hand down on the carriage or the frame beside you, the other arm long by your side."

> "**Inhale** — reach the outside arm up to the ceiling. **Exhale** — reach it up **and over**, side-bending away."

> "**Feel the ribs open on the stretched side.** That's the intention — open, not collapse."

> "**Inhale** — come back up to tall. And again, up and over."

> "Three or four breaths' worth. Take your time."

> "And come up. **Other side.**"

> "Reach up… and over. Ribs opening. Breathe into that side."

`[SILENCE]`

> "And come back up to tall. **Box away** — pop it off to the side."

### Kneeling hip flexor / quad stretch · 3–5 breaths each side

`[DO]` **Non-negotiable after the lunge series.** That back-leg hip flexor worked hard on both sides. Floor version is fine if the group is unsteady — offer it before anyone wobbles.

> "Come round to face your reformer again. **Back knee down on the carriage**, front foot on the platform. Hands on the footbar posts."

> "If kneeling on the carriage feels wobbly, **do this on the floor instead** — back knee on the mat, same shape. Genuinely the same stretch."

> "Now — **tuck your tail underneath you first.** Before you go anywhere. Tuck."

> "Then sink your hips forward and let the carriage travel a little. Feel the front of that back hip."

> "**Without the tuck you'll just arch your back instead of stretching.** Tuck first, then sink."

> "And if you want more: reach the same-side arm up and over. Three breaths."

`[SILENCE]`

> "And come out. **Other side.** Tuck the tail… then sink."

> "This is the one your hips were asking for. Three more breaths."

> "And come out. **Come and lie down on your carriage.** Head up by the shoulder blocks. **Grab your ball on the way down** — last time you need it."

### Supine figure-4 · 3–5 breaths each side

> "Lie all the way back. Ball just beside you for a moment."

> "Cross your right ankle over your left thigh — **right knee opens out to the side.** Figure four."

> "Hold behind your left thigh and gently draw it in towards you. Or leave the left foot on the carriage and just let the right knee open. Both work."

> "And breathe. **Exhale to soften a little deeper** each time. Don't force it."

`[SILENCE]`

> "And release. **Other side** — left ankle over the right thigh, left knee opens."

> "Breathe into your right glute. Three breaths."

`[SILENCE]`

> "And release. Both feet down."

### Supine spinal twist · 3–5 breaths each side

> "Knees together, draw them up. Arms out wide in a T."

> "**Exhale** — let both knees fall over to the **right**. Let the left shoulder stay heavy. Look to the left if your neck's happy."

> "And breathe. Nothing to do."

`[SILENCE]`

> "**Inhale** — bring the knees back to centre. **Exhale** — over to the **left.**"

> "Shoulders heavy. Breathe."

`[SILENCE]`

> "And back to centre. Legs long, feet down, arms by your sides."

### Final breaths

> "**Ball onto your belly.** Just rest it there. Hands on it if you like."

> "Close your eyes if that's comfortable."

> "**Inhale** — feel the ball rise. **Exhale** — feel it fall."

> "That's the same breath we started with, forty-five minutes ago. It's been here the whole time."

> "Inhale… and exhale."

`[SILENCE]` **A full 20–30 seconds. Do not fill it.**

> "One more breath in your own time. Make it a big one."

> "And in your own time — roll off to one side and come up to sitting."

> "**That was a lot of legs today.** Three moves on each side, footwork at three reds, and you piked at the end of it. Well done."

> "Drink water, eat something with protein in it, and expect to feel your inner thighs tomorrow — you've earned that."

> "Thank you all. Boxes back, springs off, balls in the basket on your way out. See you next week."
