Section 1Footwork foundations
~10 min		 Double Leg Press series → Heel Raises → Prances → Single Leg Press
  • Leg press: heels → toes to footbar variation to bias calves & quads differently.
  • Heel raises: slow eccentric lower, no bouncing.
  • Prances: rolling one heel under as the other knee bends — rhythm & ankle mobility.
  • Single leg press: free leg at tabletop; watch the pelvis staying level.

Section 2Feet in straps
~8 min		 Hamstring Press → Leg Circles → Frog Press
  • Hamstring press: pelvis heavy and stable; drive from the back of the legs.
  • Leg circles: both directions, circles no bigger than the pelvis can stay still.
  • Frog press: heels together in the V — adductor squeeze on the press out.
  • Progression if the group is strong: finish with Short Spine (headrest down).

Same springs as footwork family — no spring change into this block. Heavier to advance.
Section 3Standing lower body
~12 min		 Squats → Sumo Squat → Scooter → Flying Lunge → Lateral Lunge
  • Squats → sumo: shift from parallel to wide turnout — quads into adductors/glutes.
  • Scooter (stability) paired straight into flying lunge (mobility) — the manual's stability→mobility pairing.
  • Lateral lunge: control the carriage home — no slamming.
  • Layer per side; swap sides with minimal fuss to keep flow.
  • Optional finisher for strong groups: Standing Side Splits.

Section 4Core + upper body
~9 min		 Full Plank → Front Knee Tuck (option: Pike) → Seated Row → Biceps Curl (kneeling)
  • Plank → knee tuck → pike as a layered progression; legs stay engaged so the theme carries through.
  • Seated row: posterior chain balance after all the pressing.
  • Kneeling biceps curl: hold knots to advance.
  • Footbar comes down here — one setup change for the whole block.

Cooldown/stretch~5 min Hip opener with quad stretch → Hamstring stretch (hands on footbar) → Mermaid
  • Hip opener & quad stretch: long holds for the hip flexors that just lunged.
  • Hamstring stretch: hinge from the hips, spine long.
  • Mermaid: side-body opening; finish with a few slow breaths seated.

Teaching notes

  • Spring flow: the class runs RRR → RR → R → B with the footbar up until Section 4 — minimal spring changes and one footbar move, per the manual's sequencing guidance.
  • Leg dose: three consecutive leg blocks (~30 min) — footwork (bilateral, heavy) → straps (long levers) → standing (unilateral, light) — heavy-to-light, bilateral-before-unilateral.
  • Balance: Section 4 restores core and upper body so the class still reads full-body; seated row counters the pressing bias.
  • Supine caution: pregnant clients shouldn't stay supine more than a few minutes — swap footwork/straps for side-lying leg work and use a wedge.
  • Watch-points: pelvis level in single-leg press; circles only as big as the pelvis stays quiet; control the carriage home in all lunge work.
Spring key (per the manual): R red (heavy) · B blue (medium) · Y yellow (light). Combinations read left to right, e.g. RRR = three reds, RB = red + blue.
