Reformer Class Plan — 45 min Assessment1
Exercise sequence

Warm up — footwork and bridges: 5.5 min

Spring: RR

Supine, feet on the footbar, hips width apart, ball in the arms throughout the footwork. Dynamic, all three planes. Order: Pilates V → toes → balls of the feet → one leg → heels (bridges) → Roll Up.

  • Leg Press – Pilates V, ball over the chest x 8
    • Small pulses near straight legs, knees soft x 8
  • Leg Press – toes, parallel, hip width x 8
    • Small pulses near straight legs x 8
  • Heel Raises – same feet, legs straight x 6 full range
    • Small pulses at the top x 6
  • Prances x 20 alternating – slow and even, then quicker and lighter
  • Double Leg Press – balls of the feet, parallel, hip width x 8, slow return
    • 3 counts out, 3 counts home to advance
  • Single Leg Extension – one foot on the bar, other to tabletop, neutral first x 6 each side
    • Option 1: press out and thread the ball under the tabletop leg, hand to hand
    • Option 2: extend the leg over the footbar as the carriage comes in, bend it back as you press away – flexed toes, heel into the bar
    • Knee slightly past tabletop if the ball won’t thread
  • Glute Bridge – heels on the bar, ball between the knees
    • Neutral first: tuck, arch, settle in the middle, a few ball squeezes there
    • Articulating x 3 – squeeze at the top, hold the squeeze all the way down
  • Glute Bridge 2 – hips high, press out and pull back in x 6
    • Hips dip as the carriage goes out, lift as it comes in; ball squeezed throughout
    • Park the carriage: pulses x 6 → hold, lower 10% and lift x 6 → roll down
  • Pilates Roll Up – ball down, legs long x 4, last one stays seated
    • Bent knees, hands behind the thighs, or half roll-back, to regress
    • Slower down phase to advance
    • No roll up for pregnancy, low back or osteoporosis – roll to the side and press up

Exercise sequence

Core, hands in straps: 7 min

Spring: R

Supine, straps in hands, arms above the shoulders. One red asks more of the shoulders – regress with smaller arm range and head down.

  • Arm Press Down x 8 — head down
    • Feet on the carriage to regress
    • Legs to tabletop to advance
  • Abdominal Curl x 8 — press the arms down and curl up on the same breath (no rotation)
    • Feet on the carriage, head down, press only, to regress
    • Legs to tabletop to advance
  • Curl + Single Leg Extension x 6 each side → Double Leg Extension x 6
    • Curl and hold, extend one leg at a time, then both together
    • Head down, or single leg only, to regress · lower the legs to advance
  • Set 1 – Curl + Double Leg Extension over the footbar x 4
    • Double Leg Extension under the footbar x 4
    • Hold the extension
    • Legs only — bend and extend x 4, arms and curl stay still
    • Scissors all the way up and all the way down x 4 each side
    • Bend the knees and return to the start
  • Set 2 – Curl + Double Leg Extension, hold
    • Tricep Press in the hold x 6 — elbows bend and straighten, upper arms still
    • Hold again
    • Flutter Kick x 20 beats — small and quick
    • Return to the start

Lower body — first side: 3 min

Spring: R

Standing foot on the floor, back foot against the shoulder rest. Run both exercises on one side, then leave it. Ball in the arms throughout.

  • Long Lunge, ball held at the chest, no reach x 8
    • Lunge + reach the ball up x 8
    • 1 lunge, 1 ball pass under the front thigh, return x 6
    • Lunge hold, ball passing only x 8
    • Shorter range, hold the ball still to regress
  • Scooter, ball held in the arms, pressing straight back x 8
    • Press back + 1 standing-heel raise each rep x 8
    • Hold the back leg long, heel raises only x 8
    • Ball down, hands on the bar, heel stays down to regress
    • Slower return, deeper knee bend on the standing leg to advance

Exercise sequence

Core — plank family: 6 min

Spring: R

Ball between the knees from Down Stretch through the push-up, then down for both Elephants. Offer fists or forearms before the first exercise, not after someone is uncomfortable.

  • Down Stretch, ball between the knees x 8
    • Smaller extension, chest lifted less to regress
    • Full range, longer carriage travel to advance
  • Long Stretch, ball between the knees x 6
    • Knees down to regress
    • Full plank, longer press to advance
  • Plank, ball between the knees, hold x 3 breaths → Push-up x 8
    • Knees down to regress
    • Slower lowering to advance
  • Elephant, ball down x 8
    • Soft knees, smaller carriage travel to regress
    • Heels down, longer range to advance
  • One-Leg Elephant stretch, ball down x 5 each side
    • Shoulders square and facing forward — no hitching up on the lifted-leg side
    • Lifted foot stays low to regress
    • Longer carriage travel to advance

Lower body — second side: 3 min

Spring: R

Repeat the first side in full, same reps, same layers.

  • Long Lunge, ball at the chest x 8 + reach the ball up x 8 + 1 lunge, 1 pass x 6 + lunge hold, passing only x 8
  • Scooter with ball, straight back x 8 + heel raise x 8 + hold, heel raises only x 8

Side split — first side: 3 min

Spring: B

Standing on the reformer, facing the side: one foot on the platform at the footbar end, the other on the carriage inside the shoulder rest. Feet parallel, hips square to the side wall, weight even. Ball rides on the hips until the twist.

  • Set the stance — small press out and back x 4, hands on hips
  • Side Split, arms wide, x 8 — press out, draw back with the inner thigh
    • Smaller travel, hand on the shoulder rest to regress
    • Longer range, slower return to advance
  • Side Split + twist, ball out in front at chest height, x 6 — press out, hold, turn the ribs towards the shoulder blocks, square up before the carriage comes home
    • Ribs turn, not pelvis — hips stay square to the side wall
  • Side Split hold at mid-range — pulses x 8, then hold, heel raises x 5
    • Narrower stance, out of the range, to regress

Exercise sequence

Back chain and arms: 6 min

Spring: R

Seated or kneeling facing the straps. Straps crossed for the three rows, then uncrossed for everything after — not a spring change. Everyone stays facing the straps to the end of the block.

  • Low Row, straps crossed x 10
    • Smaller range to regress · pause at end range to advance
  • Wide Row, straps crossed, elbows to shoulder height x 10
    • Elbows lower, smaller range, sit closer to regress · pause at end range to advance
  • Single-Arm Row with Rotation, straps crossed x 8 each side
    • Sit closer, smaller rotation, or no rotation to regress · free hand reaches the opposite way to advance
  • Bicep Curl, straps uncrossed x 10 – light and rhythmic
    • Hold the last curl, then press the arms up overhead x 6
  • Mountain Climber, straps down, still facing the straps x 10
    • Plank, hands on the frame at the strap end, feet on the carriage – draw both knees in, press back out to a long plank

Side split — second side: 2.5 min

Spring: B (swapped back in, the last change — footbar down, unchanged)

Turn around, other foot on the carriage. Same stance, order, reps and regressions as Side split — first side above.

  • Set the stance x 4
  • Side Split, arms wide, x 8
  • Side Split + twist, ball out in front, x 6 — towards the shoulder blocks, so the other direction
  • Side Split hold at mid-range — pulses x 8, then heel raises x 5

Cool down: 6 min

Spring: B (unchanged — footbar down, and nothing changes again)

Static holds only, calm voice, minimal cueing, music noticeably down. Two stretches lying on the carriage in a single strap, then Mermaid, then everyone steps off and finishes standing.

  • Mermaid, side-seated on the carriage, reach up and over x 5 breaths each side
  • Hip opener, hamstring stretch on the carriage, front splits x 5 seconds hold
  • Step off — Tricep stretch, elbow up by the ear, hand down the spine, gentle press, x 5 breaths each side
  • Forward fold – roll down, knees soft, head and arms heavy x 5 breaths
  • Swing left to right in the fold, then roll up to stand tall x 20 sec
  • Deep breath – arms sweep wide and up on the inhale, float down on the exhale x 3 breaths
